The work

Three builds, in the order they mattered.

01

Chassis & geometry

Seam-welded, measured on a jig, corner-weighted with the driver in the seat. Everything downstream is guesswork until this is true.

  • Jig-measured to 0.5 mm
  • Corner-weighted loaded
  • Documented, photographed
02

Drivetrain

Built on the bench, not in the bay. Every clearance written down, every torque figure signed off by the person who turned the wrench.

  • Bench-assembled
  • Dyno-verified
  • Signed build sheet
03

Finish

Paint is the last 5% and the first thing anyone sees. Blocked flat, cut back by hand, left to settle before it goes anywhere near daylight.

  • Blocked flat
  • Hand cut & polished
  • Six-week cure

How it runs

Four stages. You see all of them.

  1. 01

    Strip and assess

    The car comes apart before we quote the real number. What we find is photographed and sent to you the same week.

  2. 02

    Fixed scope

    One document: what is being done, what it costs, when it ends. Changes are priced before they happen, not after.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly photographs from the bay. You can walk in and look at your own car on any working day.

  4. 04

    Shakedown

    Six hundred kilometres on our plates before yours go back on. Anything that moves, settles or leaks gets found by us.
