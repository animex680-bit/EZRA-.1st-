# Content & Social — Rules
## Instagram Feed, Reels, Thumbnails, Social Posts

---

## Platform Specs

| Format | Dimensions | Duration | Notes |
|--------|-----------|---------|-------|
| IG Reel | 1080×1920 | 15–90s | Vertical, 24fps or 30fps |
| IG Feed (square) | 1080×1080 | — | Consider carousel series |
| IG Feed (portrait) | 1080×1350 | — | Taller = more feed space |
| IG Story | 1080×1920 | 15s | Interactive elements |
| YouTube Thumbnail | 1280×720 | — | Must read at 150px |
| LinkedIn Post | 1200×628 | — | Horizontal dominant |

**IG Safe Zones (Reels)**:
```
Top:    14% — below story progress bars and avatar
Bottom: 18% — above caption, like, comment controls
Sides:  4%
```

---

## Stream Selection

### Instagram Feed (Static)
Primary Stream A — editorial, sculptural, stop-scroll through stillness.
- Dark editorial imagery
- Single powerful subject — no busy collages
- Minimal text overlay — one word or phrase maximum
- Series: 3 or 6-frame carousel with consistent left-edge visual thread

### Instagram Reels
Blend: A 50% / B 50%
- Strong first frame (0–1.5s decides if they keep watching)
- Kinetic typography synced to voice or music
- Motion graphics that ADD without OBSCURING the presenter
- Clear visual hierarchy: subject > supporting graphic > label

## Color Grading

**Stream A Grade** (editorial, moody):
```
Shadows:    Teal lift (+14 blue, +6 green in shadows)
Midtones:   Slightly desaturated (-10 to -20)
Highlights: Preserve or slightly warm (+5 red)
Blacks:     Crush (-6 to -10)
Contrast:   +6 to +10
Vignette:   20-30% opacity, large radius
Result:     Rich, dark, teal-shadow editorial
```

**Stream B Grade** (clean tech/product):
```
Whites:     Clean, not blown (stay under 95%)
Blacks:     Deep with blue undertone
Saturation: Normal to +10
Contrast:   +5
Temperature:Neutral to slightly cool (-3 to -5)
Result:     Clean, precise, premium tech
```

---

## Typography on Social

### Feed Posts
- ONE message, huge, covering 20–40% of frame
- Stream A: Cormorant italic | Stream B: Inter bold
- Color: Bone white (#F0EFE8) — never pure white
- No more than 8 words total

### Subtitle Style (Reels)
```
Font:     Inter Medium or Helvetica Neue Medium
Size:     36–42px
Color:    #FFFFFF with bg rgba(0,0,0,0.5)
Radius:   6px pill
Position: Bottom safe zone, horizontally centered
```

---

## Thumbnail Design

1. Readable at 150px wide (test this)
2. One clear subject (face or key visual)
3. 3 words maximum if using text
4. High-contrast text (white or yellow on dark)
5. Not clickbait — our brand is premium and real

**Style**:
- Dark gradient overlay bottom (opacity 0→0.7)
- Subject: top 60% of frame
- Text: bottom 25%, large and clean
- One brand color accent

---

## Feed Rhythm — "THE GALLERY"

- Consistent aspect ratio (portrait 4:5 = 1080×1350)
- 3-image planning: how do 3 adjacent posts look together?
- No post the same as previous 3
- Dark posts alternate with slightly lighter for rhythm
- Consistent grade across all posts

## Reels — "THE STATEMENT"

- Opening frame: dark, one face or visual + silence, then motion hits
- Middle (15–30s): kinetic typography or graphics at peak engagement
- End: clear verbal/visual CTA, simple, no cluttered graphic

---

## Anti-Patterns

- White background content
- Fonts smaller than 24px in Reels
- Text in safe-zone danger areas
- More than 3 text styles per frame
- Gradient backgrounds without a subject
- Graphics over face of speaking subject
- Anything that looks like a Canva template

---

## Production Quality Check

- [ ] First frame grabs attention within 0.5 seconds
- [ ] Safe zones respected
- [ ] Audio normalized (-14 LUFS for Instagram)
- [ ] Caption added (80% of Reels watched muted)
- [ ] Color grade applied consistently
- [ ] All text legible at 375px preview
- [ ] Aspect ratio correct
- [ ] No linear motion — all animations use easing
