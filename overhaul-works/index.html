    07 — About the shop

    ONE ROOF,
    EVERY BOLT.

    A working performance shop in the Floodgate Works — not a showroom with a kettle.

    We started in 2014 with one ramp and a welder. Today it's eight bays, our own dyno, a paint booth and a fabrication shop — all under one roof.

    Nothing gets farmed out, so nothing goes missing between trades. The person who strips your car is in the building when it's painted, mapped and signed off. Photographs every Friday, a parts list you can check line by line, and every original part back in a crate.
