About

ONE ROOF,
EVERY BOLT.

A working performance shop in Digbeth — not a showroom with a kettle. Fourteen full builds a year, the rest servicing and bodywork.

We started in 2014 with one ramp and a welder. We now run eight bays, our own dyno, a paint booth and a fabrication shop — all under one roof in the Floodgate Works.

Nothing gets farmed out, so nothing goes missing between trades. The person who strips your car is in the building when it's painted, mapped and signed off. You get photographs every Friday and a parts list you can check line by line.

Every car leaves with its build folder: the dyno sheet, the torque log, the geometry print-out, and a crate holding every original part we took off it.
