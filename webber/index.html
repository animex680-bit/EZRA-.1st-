The method

No pitch deck. A link.

It is the only honest way to sell a website. You cannot judge a rendering, a mood board or a list of adjectives. You can judge the real thing in ten seconds on your own phone.

  1. 01

    We study your business

    What you sell, who buys it, and what your current site fails to do. No questionnaire, no discovery fee.

  2. 02

    We build it

    A real site under your own name, deployed and reachable. Not a template with your logo dropped in.

  3. 03

    You open the link

    On your phone, in front of whoever you want. That is the entire pitch.

  4. 04

    You decide

    Keep it and we finish it properly, with your copy, your photography and your domain. Or walk away.

What you get

Built to be fast, found and contacted.

01

Real 3D, not fake depth

Genuine WebGL — geometry, lighting and reflection solved every frame on your visitor's device. The shapes on this page are mathematics, not pictures. Nothing here is a video loop.

  • Hand-written shaders
  • No 3D model files to download
  • Whole page under 100 KB
02

It works on the phone it is opened on

Resolution scales to the device, the frame rate is watched and quality steps down if it has to, and if a device cannot do 3D at all it still gets a complete, good-looking page.

  • Mobile-first, tested
  • Graceful fallback, never a blank screen
  • Respects reduced-motion
03

Able to actually get you work

A phone link, an email link, clear things to click, and correct preview cards so the page looks like something when you paste it into a message. Most beautiful sites fail here.

  • One tap to call
  • Proper link previews
  • Accessible, keyboard-navigable
